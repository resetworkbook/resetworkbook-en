The RESET Method

Before organizing, make space.

The method follows five steps: recognize what takes up space, eliminate what you no longer need, simplify, create balance and transform what remains.

R — RECOGNIZE

Look at what takes up space without trying to fix it right away.

E — ELIMINATE

Remove what you no longer need, even if you've learned to live with it.

S — SIMPLIFY

Reduce steps, decisions and complexity where possible.

E — EASE

Leave margin between what you have to do and how you want to live.

T — TRANSFORM

Build a more sustainable way to manage what remains.
