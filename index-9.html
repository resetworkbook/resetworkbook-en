The Method RESET

Before you organize, make space.

The Journey follows five steps: recognize what takes up space, eliminate what no longer serves you, simplify, create ease, and transform what remains.

R — RECOGNIZE

Look at what is taking up space without trying to solve it immediately.

E — ELIMINATE

Remove what you no longer need, even if you have learned to live with it.

S — SIMPLIFY

Reduce steps, decisions, and complexity wherever possible.

E — EASE

Leave margin between what you have to do and how you want to live.

T — TRANSFORM

Build a more sustainable way of managing what remains.

At the End of the Journey

30-Day Reset Challenge

Thirty days are not about becoming a new person. They are about experiencing what happens when, for one month, you choose to make a little more space.

If you miss a day, do not start over: keep going.
